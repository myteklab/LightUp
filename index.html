How to Use LightUp

Create glowing LED art just like the classic Lite-Brite toy!

  • Select a color from the palette at the top
  • Click or drag on the grid to place colored LEDs
  • Use the eraser (X button) to remove LEDs
  • Toggle power ON to see your creation glow!
  • Change grid size for larger or smaller canvases

Tips:

  • Try creating patterns, letters, or pictures
  • Mix colors for a rainbow effect
  • The glow effect looks best with power ON in a dark room!
